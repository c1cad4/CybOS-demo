# CybGrowth + Proof of Positive Impact — v0.1

Status: design proposal; no reputation scoring or rewards deployed.

## Purpose

Help people learn, build, contribute and recognize useful work in cybOS. Support domain-specific evidence of performance for humans, AI agents, robots, service nodes and ecological projects. Never assign a universal score to a person's worth or use scores as a proxy for civil rights, social standing or access to basic services.

## Core principles

- Opt-in for people; private by default; export, correction, contestation and deletion of personal profiles.
- Separate learning progress, verified contributions, operational reliability and safety compliance. No single universal leaderboard.
- No points for screen time, compulsive engagement, popularity, wealth or number of devices.
- Achievements require attributable evidence, independent review where feasible, expiry and revocation where appropriate.
- Reputation is scoped by domain and context; a high score in one domain grants no authority in another.
- Agents and robots must not self-approve their own results. Their authorization remains governed by independent capability policies, not reputation.
- Public chain holds optional privacy-preserving attestations/commitments, not identifiable learning history or raw activity.

## Profiles

Human: optional learning goals, skill portfolios, demonstrated projects and voluntary contributions; no social worth score.
AI agent: benchmark accuracy, calibrated uncertainty, reproducibility, policy compliance and independent task evaluations.
Robot: task success, maintenance and safety history, human-supervised outcomes; never incentivize unsafe operation.
Service node: verified delivery, integrity, reliability and dispute resolution.
Ecological project: evidence-backed outcomes, follow-up observations and transparent uncertainty.

## CybGrowth learning loop

Choose personal goal -> study/practice -> build a useful artifact -> seek optional feedback -> publish an evidence-backed achievement -> reflect and set next goal. Provide self-paced learning without streak pressure or punishment for breaks.

## Anti-abuse

Unique evidence IDs, signed attestations, anti-replay, reviewer conflict-of-interest checks, independent sampling, appeals, rate limits and sybil resistance. Never make payment a prerequisite for earning recognition. Keep token compensation separate from educational achievements and from safety permissions.

## MVP

Local-first private learning journal, goal tracker, skill evidence entries, optional contribution attestations and separate reliability metrics for machine nodes. No blockchain posting by default. Test with synthetic data before any real user reputation rollout.
